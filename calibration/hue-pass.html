QR65 — Saturated hue pass

Match the hue of each reference using ConneX. There are 12 saturated hues and three repeat checks. A brighter or darker appearance is acceptable for this pass; note it rather than changing speaker brightness.

Before you start
  1. Release daemon control with edifier-qr65 release, then connect ConneX.
  2. Select static lighting and set speaker brightness to 50%.
  3. Disable display night light/color filters. Keep monitor brightness, room lighting, and viewing position stable.
  4. Adjust the app color until the speaker hue looks closest to the reference. You may use any position on the app wheel, including its interior.
  5. Enter the app HEX, choose match quality, and continue. Judge repeat samples afresh.

This page displays references only; it does not control the speaker. Local progress saving is best-effort: export a checkpoint before closing.

Target: · HSV saturation/value: 100%

Empty samples can be skipped and exported as incomplete. Malformed HEX must be corrected or cleared.

When all 15 samples are complete, export and share the JSON. Disconnect ConneX and run edifier-qr65 resume to restore daemon control. White compensation and pastel preference trials come afterward.
